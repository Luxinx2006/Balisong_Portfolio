Activity 1

Who Am I?

I am someone who strives to be kind, dependable, and always learning. My personality balances thoughtfulness with action — I reflect deeply before stepping forward to help. What makes me unique is how I blend creativity with practicality: I dream big but also focus on small, daily steps. Writing, volunteering, and organizing events are my passions; they let me turn care into action.

Personality

Curious, warm, resilient

I am naturally empathetic — listening deeply to others comes easily to me. What makes me unique is my ability to find hope even in difficult moments; I don't just see problems, I look for solutions.

Interests

Writing, service, leadership

I love creative writing, community service, and learning new skills like public speaking and basic leadership. These interests let me express myself and contribute to others.

Shaping experience

Family through hardship

Seeing my parents work hard through financial hardship taught me discipline and gratitude. Having received help, I now give freely.

Shaping experience

Leading a class project

Organizing peers taught me patience and that leadership means serving, not just directing — this built my confidence and teamwork skills. Tutoring younger students later showed me that lifting others lifts me too.

Motivation

Family and future

My family is my biggest inspiration — their sacrifices push me to excel. My goal is to finish my degree, serve my community, and become a leader who lifts others up.
